02 / PROJECTS

Project archive

Projects are maintained as expandable research records. Open one to see its question, contribution, and methods; future work can be added without lengthening the homepage.

01
GEN / 2D · 2023 — 2024

Controllable image-based structure generation

Open project +
Research question

A hybrid generative system that produces 2D mechanical structures satisfying target properties and designer preferences.

Contribution

Proposed the CVAE–DDPM architecture, guidance-mask interaction, ensemble labeling strategy, and an A*-based path generation method.

Methods

CVAE · DDPM · Ensemble learning · A*

02
SUR / FEA · 2023 — 2024

Stress-field prediction and optimization with surrogate models

Open project +
Research question

A graph-enhanced convolutional surrogate for mesh-level stress and strain prediction in place of costly nonlinear finite-element simulations.

Contribution

Integrated U-Net, GraphSAGE, and SE-ResNet; introduced geometric deformation loss, LoRA transfer, and RBF contour optimization.

Methods

GNN · U-Net · GraphSAGE · LoRA · RBF

03
ROB / DIFF · 2022 — 2023

Differentiable surrogate models for soft-robot design

Open project +
Research question

A differentiable prediction pipeline for jointly optimizing the structure and trajectory of pneumatic soft robots.

Contribution

Developed a re-entrant-cuff robot and used U-Net and MeshGraphNet surrogates to replace repeated finite-element analyses.

Methods

MeshGraphNet · U-Net · Differentiable optimization

04
GNN / TRUSS · 2024

Graph-based prediction and generation of truss structures

Open project +
Research question

A spatially aware graph-learning framework for nonlinear truss deformation and adjustable-complexity structure generation.

Contribution

Proposed SA-GNN to reduce overfitting and data demand, together with a conditional graph variational autoencoder for generation.

Methods

SA-GNN · CGVAE · Nonlinear mechanics

05
INV / MDN · 2020 — 2021

Generative inverse design of auxetic metamaterials

Open project +
Research question

An inverse-design framework that generates diverse parametric structures for target stiffness, stress, and relative density.

Contribution

Combined a mixture-density generative model with an MLP surrogate to automate design generation and reduce computational cost.

Methods

MDN · MLP · Inverse design · Abaqus

06
LLM / CAD · 2023 — 2024

Large language models in engineering design education

Open project +
Research question

An investigation of how language models affect concept generation and detailed CAD modeling in engineering education.

Contribution

Designed a progressive prompting approach to improve the reliability of LLM responses in engineering-design contexts.

Methods

LLM · Progressive prompting · CAD · Design education
